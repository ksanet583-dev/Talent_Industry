Building a Stronger Industrial Future for Ethiopia

Talent Industrial Technology PLC is an Ethiopian company focused on manufacturing and supplying quality industrial and infrastructure products. We provide solutions for different sectors, including energy, telecommunications, water supply, irrigation, and construction. Established in accordance with Ethiopia's Commercial Code, the company operates its manufacturing facilities in Addis Ababa. Through our commitment to quality, reliable products, and customer satisfaction, we aim to support Ethiopia's growing infrastructure and industrial development.

Our Mission

our mission is to provide high-quality industrial and engineering products that help build stronger, more sustainable infrastructure. From telecom towers and electrical utility materials to irrigation equipment, industrial spare parts, and construction materials, we are dedicated to meeting our customers' needs through innovation, quality workmanship, and reliable service. We believe in building lasting relationships by understanding our customers, delivering on our promises, and maintaining high professional standards in everything we do.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become one of the leading manufacturers and suppliers of industrial and engineering products in Ethiopia and across East Africa. We aim to grow by providing dependable, innovative, and high-quality solutions that make a meaningful difference in the industries and communities we serve. Through trust, professionalism, continuous improvement, and strong partnerships, we aspire to be a company our customers can rely on today and in the future.
