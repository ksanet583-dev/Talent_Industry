Building Ethiopia's Critical Infrastructure

We specialize in manufacturing and supplying industrial solutions across three vital sectors that power Ethiopia's development and growth.

>Electrical Power systems

We build strong and reliable structures that help deliver electricity across Ethiopia. Our work includes manufacturing transmission towers, distribution poles, and substation equipment to support the country's growing power needs.

  • High-Voltage Transmission Towers
  • Electricity Distribution Poles
  • Substation Equipment

>Telecommunication Systems

We manufacture reliable structures that support mobile networks and communication services across Ethiopia. Our products include telecom towers, monopoles, and antenna mounting structures designed to meet the growing demand for better connectivity.

  • Telecom Towers
  • Monopoles
  • Antenna Mounting Structures

>Water Supply Systems

We produce quality water and irrigation solutions that help support agricultural activities and community water needs. Our products include pipelines, storage tanks, and irrigation equipment for both agricultural and municipal projects.

  • Water Supply Pipelines
  • Water Storage Tanks
  • Irrigation Systems
